# Boards know nothing of connectors

A board (the Jira board) only reads its own tool and offers an issue to whatever is listening; it never names Lific or any other destination. Taking a Jira issue into Lific is a separate own mod, a connector, that reads the issue from Jira itself and writes its tracking issue in Lific. The person asked for this layering: Jira is the core and stays read-only, Lific is where an issue is worked and linked to others, and either side should keep working, or be switched off, without the other. We rejected teaching the Jira mod about Lific (every new destination would grow the board) and having the Lific mod read Jira (it would need Jira's credentials and reads).

**Consequences:** handing an issue to an agent session stays agnostic: each board hands what it holds (a Jira issue from the Jira board, a Lific issue from Lific). A bring-in happens only when asked and updates only what Jira owns (title, description, Jira's status), never the tracking issue's Lific status, comments or links. The contract between a board and its connectors, what the board publishes and how a connector registers, is the connector's design and is recorded with it.
